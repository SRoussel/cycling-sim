# Cycling Race Simulator

## Project Overview
A cycling race simulator in Go that produces realistic race results for men's WorldTour and ProSeries level racing. Uses a hybrid physics-based model with abstract ability modifiers.

## Build & Test
Speed (Nov 2026, the user: slow test runs are a concern): a Liège + a Giro run took 25.6 s, now 13.8 s. Profile: `data/local/zz_prof_test.go.txt` (BenchmarkZZRaces, ZZ_PROF=1 ZZ_GT=1 with -cpuprofile). Exact changes only: the speed solver is a bracketed Newton (`solveSpeed`; same speeds to 5e-9 m/s over 200k cases, was ~45% of time as bisection), gravity/rolling without trig, `DraftFactor` from a table, GC places ranked once per stage; the race-shape check reuses the GC validation runs (`ShapesFromSummaries`). Rejected: caching `sprinterCanFollow` across steps (approximate, and the bunch's make-up changes nearly every step so it rarely hit). `validate --quick`: 2026, one seed set, a third of the runs, for iterating.

```bash
go build ./cmd/cycling-sim/       # build binary
go test ./...                      # run all tests
go test ./internal/simulation/ -v  # verbose simulation tests
```

Requires Go 1.24+ and uses `modernc.org/sqlite` (pure Go, no CGO needed).

## Project Structure
- `main.go`, `app.go`, `interactive_*.go` — Wails desktop app backend (package main); `gui/` is the React/Vite frontend
- `cmd/cycling-sim/main.go` — CLI entry point
- `cmd/dbcheck/` — small DB inspection utility
- `internal/service/` — race loading, start-list selection, running and saving; shared by CLI and GUI (use it, don't duplicate)
- `internal/seed/` — seed data (riders from `data/riders.csv`, courses, races, stage races)
- `internal/selection/` — start-list selection per race profile
- `internal/calibrate/` — `fit-ratings`: fits rider thresholds to real results by simulation
- `internal/stagefile/` — reads Pro Cycling Manager stage files (`.cds`) installed locally, converts route profile + cobbled/gravel sectors into segments
- `internal/model/` — Domain structs (Rider, Course, Segment, Race, Result, GCStanding) with validation
- `internal/simulation/` — Core simulation engine:
  - `physics.go` — Speed-from-power solver (bisection method), drag/gravity/rolling forces, drafting
  - `fatigue.go` — W' balance (within-stage) + cumulative multi-stage fatigue
  - `peloton_*.go` — groups (`peloton_follow.go`), effort/teams (`peloton_groups.go`), steps (`peloton_segment.go`)
  - `wind.go`, `altitude.go` — wind/echelons, climb factor, altitude; `PrepareSegments` readies a course for a race
  - `tactics.go` — Breakaway logic, sprint finish resolution, daily form variance
  - `engine.go`, `runner.go` — Orchestrate road races, TTs, and stage races (runner adds progress callbacks)
  - `validation.go` — synthetic fields; `validation_real.go` — 2024 references + 2023 hold-out; `validation_speed.go` — winning speeds
- `internal/database/` — SQLite schema, migrations, CRUD operations

## Key Design Decisions
Guiding rule: express behaviour as physics or rider intent, never as hardcoded gradient/distance thresholds.

- **Ability modifiers** (1-100 scale, 70 = WT average): `effectivePower = base * (1 + (ability-70)*0.002)`, giving ±4% range. W/kg (FTP/weight) is the main performance lever. Climbing ability blends in with the climb factor (full effect on real climbs, none on the flat). `EffectivePower` is the rider's threshold on that terrain *and* the W' threshold (`thresholdIntOn`) — keep them the same, or riding "at threshold" drains/refills W' and cancels abilities.
- **Speed solver**: bisection on `0.5·ρ·CdA·(v+w)|v+w|·v + staticForce·v = P` (w = headwind; robust on descents).
- **Steps**: segments are simulated in ≤2 km steps; tactical/incident probabilities are per 5 km (`ScaleToDistance`). One-day races after the early phase (last 60 km) use 250 m steps (`fineStepKm`, `simulateFine`) with a 3 s contact range (`fineContactSeconds`): with short steps the 10 s range hands a dropped rider their gap back every 20 s (a synthetic mountain race's climbing order fell from Spearman 0.98 to 0.40). Wind meander stays on the coarse-step scale (`stepHeadingAt`). Stage races are unchanged.
- **Groups** (`peloton_follow.go`): one rule on all terrain. Pace comes from a rotating pace line of workers (riders chasing/attacking/driving; small groups share). A big group with nobody working holds a collective tempo set by its middle riders (`tempoRiders`), not its strongest, until the finale. Followers must hold it with positional draft + spendable W'; else they're dropped. Groups line out at ≥95% threshold (no deep bunch shelter). Cooperative chases of a break spend W' N/k faster (pool depth). Groups can't pass each other; riders in a group close up to its front.
- **Contact**: riders within 10 s are one road group. Breakaway state is read from the road.
- **Effort** (`riderEffort`): each mode draws on W' once over one horizon — attack: the step; committed chase / finale (last 20 km): to the line; team driving: rest of the climb. Domestiques pace at threshold until the 3 km lead-out.
- **Short-effort ceiling** (Nov 2026, `power_ceiling.go`, shown by the rider panel: Vingegaard out-produced Philipsen over 30 s, 19.3 vs 16.7 W/kg): above threshold a rider rides threshold + W' spread over the effort, but never more than MAP + (sprint − MAP)·e^(−(t−5 s)/40 s) (MAP = threshold × (1 + punch gap)); every burst (attacks, following, chases, racing to the line, leader choice) goes through `maxPowerFor`. 2026 check (--sets 2): near neutral, a little closer to real on the hilly classics: Amstel solo 73 -> 62% (real a small group), margin 0'32" -> 0'20", Flèche 69 -> 56%, 0'35" -> 0'27", Lombardia margin 1'13" -> 0'59" (0'48"), one-day solo 59 -> 57% (36%); Milan-San Remo gap 70 -> 116 s; gap sum 9404 -> 9348 s; stage races unchanged. Kept (physiology).
- **W'**: anaerobic capacity, 30% from peak power (14 s × sprint watts) and 70% from threshold (54.8 s × FTP), ~17–25 kJ (Nov 2026; before, all from peak power, which gave pure sprinters the biggest reserves on short climbs). Depletes above FTP; recovers with Skiba (2012) τ = 546·e^(−0.01·(CP−P)) + 316 s.
- **Durability**: in-race threshold fades ~2.5%/1000 kJ (Endurance-scaled, cap 15%); since Nov 2026 W' max fades by the same factor (`addWork`; W' falls after prolonged exercise as CP does). Races are not too easy in energy: a sim Paris-Roubaix costs the field a median ~7300 kJ (real ~5500-6500 kJ), Lombardia ~6000, Flanders ~7200. They are too uniform: the bunch rides a steady ~75% of threshold, which never touches W', and few riders reach the 15% cap (raising it to 30% changed nothing). W' fade, --sets 3: gap sum 1905 -> 1927 s, solo 38 -> 40%, Giro 143 -> 117 s, Tirreno 227 -> 245 s; Milan-San Remo 2024 solo 28 -> 53%: accepted with the user, the race's recent history is about half solo (2021-2023) and half small-group sprints (2020, 2024, 2025), so judge it on that mix, not on the 2024 edition alone. Surges (Nov 2026, `accordionCost`): when a group speeds up, a rider at depth d in the line (0 front, 1 back) pays the extra kinetic energy of an amplified speed-up from W' (`accordionGain` 1: the back sees twice the front's change), so riders at the back burn matches all day. --sets 3: solo 40 -> 45%, Roubaix 2024 solo 2 -> 12% (bunch 58 -> 52%), Grand Tour breakaways ~6.3 -> 7.1 (real 7.5) and selections ~8.1 -> 7.3 (real 5.5), Giro 2nd 11'24" -> 9'56" (real 9'56"), Catalunya 2nd/10th closer; gap sum 1927 -> 2169 s (Liège 2024 115 -> 287: its real top-10 puncheurs get worn down; Catalunya 675 -> 774). - **Altitude**: elevation integrated from gradients (course low point = 0 m); air density by standard atmosphere; aerobic power by Bassett et al. 1999 (acclimatised): ~92% at 2000 m.
- **Drafting**: `DraftFactor` is a smooth curve: 0.65 on one wheel → 0.25 deep in a bunch.
- **Stage fatigue**: `cumulativeFatigue += stress·(1 − recovery/150)`, FTP × (1 − 0.004·fatigue), floor 85% (~4% over a Grand Tour), always applied to the rider's base FTP (a bug once compounded it stage on stage, ~40% by Paris). Time cuts by climbing density (waived if >20% outside).
- **Wind**: segments have headings (synthetic in seed data); headwind enters the solver; crosswind removes gutter shelter beyond a 10-rider echelon by yaw angle; roads meander ±60° per step.
- **Terrain**: `ClimbFactor` (share of power lifting weight) classifies terrain for tactics.
- **Team strategy**: a team drives when its leader's threshold pace would break the group's fastest finisher even in the draft; its domestiques then set the pace the leader can hold on the wheel at the phase intensity (`leaderTempoSpeed`: a tempo mid-race, threshold in the finale), not an all-out effort. Leaders race in the finale; until then they sit in, even during chases, while a teammate is in their group: a leader with a rival up the road is chased for by their domestiques and only takes turns alone.
- **Stage races** (`AssignCoLeaders`, `gcRivalAhead`): each team's GC leader is its best climber by W/kg (not the ability-rating average `AssignTeamRoles` uses); domestiques who are among the 10 strongest climbers in the field, or in the GC top 10, become co-leaders. A GC leader with a rival up the road who would pass them on GC has their domestiques chase it to the line (alone, they chase themselves).

## Race control (Nov 2026)
Agreed with the user: bunch behaviour from team interest, not chase rules (`chase.go`), in stage races and one-day races.
- Planned chase: a team chases a break only if it threatens what it rides for. A GC contender's team defends its leader's place, only a top-10 place (`gcPlacesProtected`): the break may gain until its best-placed rider would pass the leader on GC. A sprint team chases a stage its sprinter can contest (`sprinterCanFollow`: holds the pace of the riders fastest on each remaining segment, with one W' tank over the run-in). A favourite's team hunts the stage when it decided to this morning and a steady 85% tempo catches the break (`stageHuntMax`). Only teams whose allowance an easy bunch wouldn't respect chase; the tightest of those sets the lowest pace-line intensity that keeps the break within it at the line (projected `timeToLineAt`); only their domestiques chase, leaders/sprinters sit in.
- Sprint chases in stage races (Nov 2026, Giro 2026 trace, data/local/zz_giro26_test.go.txt with the `traceChase` hook): the Giro's breaks were chased all day on hilly and mountain stages by teams of puncheurs the model calls sprinters (UAE for Narváez, Uno-X for Holter, Pinarello for F. Christen: `sprinterCanFollow` holds a threshold pace line), then Vingegaard won from the selection (breakaways 3.3, real 8; selections 10, real 5). Now a sprint team chases whatever it costs only if its sprinter also survives the favourites racing the last 20 km (`sprinterSurvivesFinale`: the fastest line at threshold + W' over each finale segment, `maxPowerFor`; true of a flat run-in); one who holds only the threshold pace has his team ride for the stage as a hunter (only when a steady tempo, `stageHuntMax`, catches the break). One-day races unchanged. --sets 2 (2026): Giro sprints 6.4 -> 6.2 (real 7), breakaways 3.6 -> 7.4 (8), selections 10.0 -> 6.4 (5), stage outcomes match 50 -> 60%, Giro 2nd 13'14" -> 10'03" (5'22"), week-long races unchanged (breakaways 0.3, real 0.5); gap sum 9348 -> 9050 s. - Place defence is affordable-only (Nov 2026): the race leader's team defends the jersey whatever it costs; teams defending places 2-10 (and, before any GC, every contender's team) join a chase only when a steady tempo (`stageHuntMax`) keeps the break within their allowance. ## Season mode (Nov 2026, the user chose full management on the seeded calendar)
`internal/season` (CLI `cycling-sim season ...`, GUI `/season`, `season_api.go`). A season is a save game with its own copy of every rider (`season_riders`: contract, salary, morale, racing load, potential, next-year signing), a calendar of the seeded races in the season's year (reference editions and stand-alone TTs left out), results with points (`season_results`, simplified UCI tables) and planned starts (`season_entries`). Races never touch `race_results`.
- Programmes (`planYear`, at the start of each year): riders take targets first, (rider, event) pairs by prestige × fit (`fits`: standard deviations above the field on the race; a stage race's GC profile is its mountain and TT stages, its stage fit the third-best stage), only events within 65% of the rider's best fit (`targetShare`; Van der Poel targets the cobbles, not the Ardennes), a second Grand Tour at half want; then each event's field is filled (teams in it complete their squads, the best-suited other teams take the places; leaders, value >= 1.5, ride only their targets). Constraints (`schedule.fits`): no overlapping events, load <= `RestLoad` at every start, <= 2 Grand Tours, race days <= `riderBudget` (75, down to 53 for the best riders). The player's team rides every event.
- Racing (`Prepare`): planned riders less retired/racing elsewhere; the player's picks replace his team's plan. Threshold × `LoadFactor` (racing load in race days, decaying with τ 14 d: free up to 6, -0.4%/day above, floor 94%) × `MoraleFactor` (±~1%); seasonal form on (`FormDate`, `SeasonalForm`).
- Race fits (`fit.go`, Nov 2026): half physics, half ratings. Physics: `simulation.HardTime` (time over the hard sections at threshold + W', as one-day leaders are picked), a time trial's `CourseTime`, the sprint (`SprintPower`/CdA) blended in as far as the hard sections are short (all of it on a course without any, none once they take the field 40 min); a stage race's GC is its mountain stages (hard sections >= 15 min for the field) plus TTs. Ratings: the selector's `RiderScore` (carries cobbles handling and the like). Physics alone ranked GT climbers far above classics stars (Van der Poel 18th); ratings alone overpaid classics domestiques (Matthews €5.7M, Roglič €4M). Blended: Pogačar, Vingegaard, Evenepoel, Van der Poel, Roglič, Van Aert at the top. Planning also caps leaders at 2 per team per race (`leadersPerEvent`: teams spread their GC riders over the Grand Tours) and a Tour-Vuelta double keeps 60% of the want (Giro-Tour 50%).
- Management: `Values` (mean of a rider's three best race fits) sets salaries (`SalaryFor`) and asks (`AskingSalary`: morale, +15% to leave a team). The board sets objectives from team strength and leaders' programmes (team rank, wins, places, stage wins), confidence moves the next budget (×0.88-1.12). `Offer`: renewals and signings for next year in the season, free agents in the off-season (budget, 30-rider cap). `EndYear`: AI renewals (top 24, <35), moves, free agents, retirements (age-based), ageing (`ageChange` × potential), neo-pros cloned from young riders 4-10% weaker; `StartYear`: AI teams sign free agents (strongest teams first, roughly), calendar, programmes, objectives. Four simulated years stay plausible (Pogačar ~20 wins a year, young riders come through, ~50 retirements a year).
- `apply-ratings` now updates ages too (real ages from WorldDB; the DB had riders.csv placeholders: Evenepoel 36).

## Frontend
- `gui/src/sim/useTeamControl.ts` holds all live-race state/commands; `gui/src/components/race/` holds the shared panels. Both `Simulation.tsx` and `StageRaceSimulation.tsx` use them.
- Typecheck with `cd gui && npx tsc --noEmit`.
- Team orders (Nov 2026): the player's standing orders (`SetTeamOrder(kind, auto|yes|no)`) reach the engine as `TeamOrders` through `TeamOrderProvider`, an optional interface of the session's `ActionOverrideProvider`; a rider's own command always wins. Chase: in `planChase` yes gives the team the tightest allowance (break back `catchMarginS` before the line), no leaves it out, and its domestiques don't chase. ## 2026-11-26: steering riders: how deep they dig, how hard they work
Most of a rider's reserve goes on following (holding the group's speed: `followSplit` lets a follower spend W' to the last), which no control touched: the effort slider only scaled the power he meant to ride. ## 2026-11-26: calibration out of sample (crossval)
